# 🎬 BolTranslate — 3-Minute Winning Demo Video Script

> **Goal:** Showcase the problem, demonstrate **Airplane Mode (Offline On-Device)** translation, demonstrate **Cloud Gemini** translation, and show the **Split-Screen Conversation Mode** in under 3 minutes.
> You can record using OBS Studio, Loom, or your phone/screen recorder!

---

### ⏱️ Timestamp Breakdown

#### 0:00 - 0:35 | Step 1: The Problem & Pitch
- **What to say (Hindi/English mix):**
  *"Hello judges! In India, millions of travelers and rural citizens face spotty, unreliable internet. Traditional translation apps freeze and fail right when you need them most—at railway stations, during travel, or in medical emergencies. Moreover, users hesitate to send sensitive conversations over the cloud. That's why we built **BolTranslate**—a hybrid AI translation platform powered by on-device Gemini Nano and cloud Gemini."*
- **What to show on screen:**
  - Show the BolTranslate dashboard with the clean modern UI and the brand badge.

---

#### 0:35 - 1:30 | Step 2: The Magic of On-Device (Airplane Mode)
- **What to do:**
  1. Click the **"Airplane Mode"** toggle in the top navbar (or turn off your Wi-Fi).
  2. Notice the top pill instantly changes to **"Offline (Airplane Mode)"**.
  3. Click the preset button: **"⚡ Travel Phrase (Short / On-Device)"** (e.g. *"कहाँ जाना है भाई? मुझे नई दिल्ली रेलवे स्टेशन जाना है।"*) or click the **Speak** button and speak in Hindi.
  4. Click **Translate Now**.
- **What to say:**
  *"Notice that our app is in complete Airplane Mode with zero internet connection. When I enter a short everyday phrase and hit translate—BAM! In just 15 milliseconds, the translation appears! Look at this glowing green badge: '⚡ On-Device (Gemini Nano / Edge) • 14ms • 100% Private (0 bytes network egress)'. Even without internet, our local edge model delivers instant, reliable results."*
  - Click the **Listen** speaker icon to play the English audio output!

---

#### 1:30 - 2:15 | Step 3: Cloud Scalability (Long-Form Nuance)
- **What to do:**
  1. Toggle **Airplane Mode: Off** (back online).
  2. Click the preset: **"☁️ Complex Paragraph (>15 Words / Cloud Gemini)"**.
  3. Click **Translate Now**.
- **What to say:**
  *"Now let's connect back online. What happens when a user needs to translate a complex, multi-sentence article or idiom? Our smart hybrid routing engine automatically inspects the token count. Because this text exceeds 15 words, BolTranslate seamlessly routes it to Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash in the cloud. Look at the purple badge: '☁️ Cloud (Gemini 1.5 Flash) • High Precision'. You get the best of both worlds: offline edge speed for quick phrases, and deep cloud intelligence for long-form text."*

---

#### 2:15 - 2:45 | Step 4: Face-to-Face Conversation Mode
- **What to do:**
  1. Click on the **"Face-to-Face Conversation"** tab.
  2. Click Speaker 1 (Hindi) mic, speak a greeting, watch it translate to English and speak aloud.
  3. Click Speaker 2 (English) mic, speak an answer, watch it translate to Hindi and speak aloud.
- **What to say:**
  *"We also built a split-screen Face-to-Face Conversation mode. A Hindi-speaking traveler and an English-speaking shopkeeper can talk naturally. Each tap records, translates, and speaks the translation aloud automatically."*

---

#### 2:45 - 3:00 | Step 5: Telemetry Dashboard & Wrap-Up
- **What to do:**
  1. Click on the **"Hybrid Telemetry & Cost Savings"** tab.
  2. Point out the On-Device ratio, cost saved ($), and latency comparison.
- **What to say:**
  *"Finally, our Telemetry Dashboard shows real-time cost savings and edge efficiency. By processing over 70% of frequent queries locally on-device, BolTranslate slashes cloud server costs and provides an unbreakable offline safety net. Thank you!"*

---

### 💡 Video Recording Tips:
- Keep the video between **2 minutes 30 seconds and 2 minutes 50 seconds** (never exceed 3:00).
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Public).
- Make sure your microphone audio is clear and crisp.
